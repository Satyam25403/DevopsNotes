VENDOR INTEROPERABILITY
Any device from any vendor can talk to any other device — as long as both follow the OSI spec. Your iPhone connects to a Cisco router. No problem.
🔧
MODULAR & REPLACEABLE
Swap Wi-Fi for fiber at Layer 1 and nothing above changes. Upgrade TCP at Layer 4 without rewriting your browser. Each layer is independent.
🐛
EASY TO DEBUG
Network broken? Check one layer at a time. Is the cable plugged in? (Layer 1). Can you ping? (Layer 3). Can you load a page? (Layer 7). Isolate fast.
KEY IDEA
Layering = Separation of concerns
Each layer does ONE job well and hands off to its neighbor. Nobody has to know everything.
