WHY LLMNR EXISTS:
When DNS fails, Windows broadcasts LLMNR (UDP 5355) + NBT-NS (UDP 137): "Does anyone know where \\fileserver is?" — anyone on the LAN can answer.
📡 LLMNR IS ON BY DEFAULT
Windows broadcasts hostname queries to the entire LAN segment. Responder answers all of them, triggering NTLM authentication to the attacker's machine.
