EMAIL HEADER (DKIM-Signature:)
v=1; a=rsa-sha256; d=company.com; s=mail; b=Qjk4X9…
HOW DKIM WORKS:
1
Your mail server signs each email with a private key → adds DKIM-Signature header
2
Receiving server fetches your public key from DNS: selector._domainkey.domain.com
3
Signature valid = email was not tampered with in transit. If body changes → signature breaks.
VERIFY WITH DIG:
dig TXT mail._domainkey.yourco.com
"v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=MIGfMA0GCS..."
🔏 DKIM = TAMPER-PROOF SIGNATURE
Even if attacker spoofs the From header, they don't have your private key. Signature fails. DKIM also proves the body wasn't modified.

THE DMARC DNS TXT RECORD:
_dmarc.yourcompany.com TXT
"v=DMARC1; p=reject;
 rua=mailto:dmarc@yourco.com;
 pct=100"
p=reject Reject all failing emails
p=quarantine Send to spam folder
p=none Monitor only (start here)
rua= Email address for aggregate reports
⚖️ DMARC = WHAT TO DO ON FAILURE
Start with p=none to collect reports, then escalate to quarantine, then reject. Never skip to reject without testing.
