KEY DIFFERENCE: VRRP IS FASTER
VRRP advertises every 1s vs HSRP's 3s hello.
VRRP dead time ≈ 3× advert = ~3s failover.
HSRP dead time = 10s → ~14s to failover.
VRRP ≈ 3× faster failover than HSRP.
VRRP = open standard. HSRP = Cisco-only.
both use priority to elect master/active.
VRRP: 3s failover. HSRP: ~14s failover.

Attacker's priority=255 packet → rejected → attack fails.
