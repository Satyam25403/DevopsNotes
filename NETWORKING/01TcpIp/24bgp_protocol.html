RPKI: RESOURCE PUBLIC KEY INFRASTRUCTURE
RPKI lets IP address holders cryptographically sign which ASN is authorized to announce their prefix.

ROA = Route Origin Authorization
A signed certificate: "AS7018 may announce 1.2.3.0/24"

Routers with RPKI validation will reject INVALID routes — even if announced by a legitimate peer.
