STEP 3: AGING — ENTRIES EXPIRE
AGING TIMER 300s TTL
WHY AGING EXISTS
Hosts move. Laptops unplug and replug
on different ports. Without aging, the
switch would forward to the wrong port.

⚡ CONSEQUENCE
Legit MACs can't be learned.
Switch floods all frames to all ports.
Attacker's NIC sees everyone's traffic.
✓ DEFENCE: PORT SECURITY
Limit max MACs per port (e.g. 3).
Violation → port shutdown or restrict mode.
Full CAM = switch reverts to hub behaviour.
Port security limits MACs per port. Default: off.
