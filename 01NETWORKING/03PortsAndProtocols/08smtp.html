:25
SERVER→SERVER (MTA RELAY)
No auth required. ISPs block outbound :25 to prevent spam. Open relay = critical vuln.
:587
AUTHENTICATED SUBMISSION
Login required. STARTTLS upgrades to TLS. This is what email clients use. Use this.
:465
SMTPS — IMPLICIT TLS
TLS wraps session from first byte. Was deprecated, re-assigned 2018. Some clients prefer this.
:25 = highway with no ID check. :587 = toll booth.
Email clients must use :587 or :465, never :25.

SPF · DKIM · DMARC
THREE DNS RECORDS THAT STOP SPOOFING
DEFENCES DNS RECORDS
SPF
TXT RECORD
Declares which IPs are allowed to send email for this domain. Receiving server checks sender IP against list.
victim.com TXT "v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all"
+all = permit all (bad)
~all = soft fail
-all = hard fail ✓
DKIM
SIGNATURE
Cryptographic signature in email headers. Proves body wasn't tampered and was sent by domain's private key. Survives forwarding.
DKIM-Signature: v=1; a=rsa-sha256; d=victim.com; s=mail; b=Hj4f...
DMARC
POLICY
Tells receiver what to do when SPF or DKIM fails. Also sends forensic reports to domain owner. Requires both SPF and DKIM to work.
_dmarc.victim.com TXT "v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:dmarc@victim.com"
p=none (monitor) → p=quarantine → p=reject ✅
SPF = IP whitelist. DKIM = signature. DMARC = policy.
All three together stop spoofing at the receiver.
