WHICH REGISTRY SHOULD I USE?
🏭 AIR-GAPPED ENVIRONMENTS
Harbor is the CNCF standard for offline registries.
registry:2 works too but lacks RBAC and scanning.
Both run fully without internet access.
🔒 ALWAYS USE TLS IN PRODUCTION
Let's Encrypt + Caddy or nginx termination.
Harbor includes TLS out of the box.
Never use --insecure-registry on shared networks.
Local dev → registry:2. Team / prod self-hosted → Harbor. Cloud-native → ECR/GAR/GHCR.
The right registry depends on your threat model, not your familiarity with Docker Hub.
